    How we'll use this page today

    This is a working copy of the referral team's own workflow map, rebuilt so we can mark it up together and leave the room with decisions written down. Nothing here is final. The current-state map is a transcription — if it is wrong, say so and it gets fixed.

    Agenda

    1
    Confirm the current state
    Walk the map left to right. Is this what actually happens? Click any step to see what the map says and the questions we have.
    Current-state map · ~10 min
    2
    Find the gaps
    Drag the day slider. Amber means no one is doing anything on that day. Switch scenarios to follow a booked vs. unbooked referral.
    Day slider · ~5 min
    3
    React to the observations
    Twelve draft observations. For each: agree, disagree, or discuss, and a one-line response. Disagreeing is useful — it means the map is missing something you do.
    Observations tab · ~15 min
    4
    Draw the future state
    Reorder, add, and remove steps live. Give each step an owner and a day target. The coverage bar shows whether the redesign still has holes.
    Future state editor · ~20 min
    5
    Capture and close
    Everything marked is compiled as text. Copy or email it before we leave. The next version of this page is built from those notes.
    Meeting notes tab · ~5 min

    The tabs

    Current-state mapThe team's map as drawn: day bands, steps, the scheduling decision, closure triggers. Click a step for detail and to record a decision.
    ObservationsDraft discussion points tagged Gap, Ambiguity, Measurement, or Hand-off. Filter by tag or by "not yet marked".
    Future state — editorEditable step strip seeded from the current state, plus a day-coverage bar and an owner swimlane that build themselves.
    Meeting notesLive compilation of every decision, vote, note, and the redesign. Copy, download, or email.
    Change logWhat changed in each version of this page, so the team can see how it evolves between sessions.

    How to interact

    On the map
    • Click a step to open its detail panel at the side. Use ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys to walk step by step.
    • In the panel, choose Keep / Change / Drop / Discuss, pick who should own it, and type a note. The choice shows as a badge on the step.
    • Drag the day slider to see where a referral sits on any day; amber means the map has nothing for that day.
    On the observations
    • Agree / Disagree / Discuss on each one, plus a short response.
    • Use the filters to focus on one category or on what is still unmarked.
    • The counter at the top right shows progress through the twelve.
    In the editor
    • Drag the ⠿ handle or use ◀ ▶ to reorder a step.
    • ＋ on a card inserts a step after it; ✕ removes it; "Add step at end" appends.
    • Edit name, day range, owner, actions, and rationale directly on the card. The coverage bar and swimlane update as you type.
    • "Reset to current state" puts the original steps back.
    • Publish ▸ makes a clean, printable document of the redesign (flow, coverage, swimlane, table, what changed). Open it to print or save as PDF, or download the file to email.

    Ground rules

    • Everything you mark is saved only on this laptop. Before we close, someone opens Meeting notes and hits Copy or Email. That text is what the next version is built from.
    • No patient information anywhere on this page. Use "a patient" or a made-up example, never a name, MRN, or date of birth, in notes.
    • "Discuss" is a valid answer. If we cannot settle something in the room, mark it Discuss with a note on who will find out.
    • The Feedback button at the bottom right sends a note to Mike about the page itself, separate from the meeting notes.
    Reopen any time with the ? button at the top right · Esc closes
